About OFFER OS

Evidence-Driven Product Launch System

What is OFFER OS?

OFFER OS is a practical system designed to help creators, founders, and product builders turn ideas into clearer, evidence-driven offers.

How it works

OFFER OS helps you organize your thinking around a product idea, challenge assumptions, collect evidence, build an offer, design experiments, and make better-informed decisions.

Local-First Approach

The current version of OFFER OS is designed as a local-first tool. Project information can be stored locally in your browser, allowing you to work with your information directly on your device.

Our Goal

The goal of OFFER OS is simple: make the process of developing and testing product ideas more structured, practical, and evidence-driven.

Important Note

OFFER OS is a productivity and decision-support tool. It does not guarantee that a product, business idea, or offer will succeed.
