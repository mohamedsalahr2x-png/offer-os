Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 18, 2026

OFFER OS respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the OFFER OS website.

1. Information You Enter

OFFER OS allows you to enter information such as project names, ideas, assumptions, evidence, offers, experiments, and decision records.

In the current version of OFFER OS, project information is stored locally in your browser on your device. It is not automatically sent to an OFFER OS server.

2. Local Storage

Information saved through the application may remain in your browser until you delete it, clear your browser data, or otherwise remove the stored information.

3. Personal Information

The current core version of OFFER OS does not require you to create an account or provide personal information to use the main application.
